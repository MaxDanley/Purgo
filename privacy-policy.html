1. Information We Collect

Frequently Asked Questions

❓ General Questions

Q: Do I need to create an account to use Purgo?

A: No! All core features (session tracking, timers, achievements, statistics) work without an account. Accounts are only needed for social features like friends and leaderboards.

Q: Is my session data private?

A: Yes, your session data is stored locally on your device. Only aggregated statistics (total time, session count) are shared if you use social features.

Q: Can I use Purgo offline?

A: Absolutely! The timer and all tracking features work completely offline. Internet is only needed for social features.

Q: How do Live Activities work?

A: When you start a session, Purgo creates a Live Activity that shows your timer on the lock screen and Dynamic Island (iPhone 14 Pro and newer).

🔐 Privacy Questions

Q: What data do you collect?

A: Without an account: Only local session data stored on your device. With an account: Email, display name, profile picture (from Google), and aggregated session statistics for leaderboards.

Q: Do you share my data with third parties?

A: We only use Google Sign-In for authentication and Firebase for secure data storage. We never sell or share your personal information with other third parties.

Q: How can I delete my data?

A: Local data: Delete the app. Account data: Sign out from the app or contact support for permanent deletion.

🏥 Health & Safety

Q: Is sauna and cold therapy safe?

A: Thermal therapies can have health benefits but also risks. Always consult healthcare professionals before starting, especially with pre-existing conditions. Start slowly and listen to your body.

Q: What are recommended session durations?

A: This varies greatly by individual. Beginners might start with 5-10 minutes, while experienced users may go 20+ minutes. Always prioritize safety over duration goals.
